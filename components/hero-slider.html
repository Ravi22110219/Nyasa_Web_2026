Education for All Children

Providing quality education to underprivileged children across rural communities and kids of construction workers in and around the campus under the Chetana programme.

Our Activities

Annual Summer Camp

Nyasa hosts an annual summer camp at IIT Gandhinagar, bringing together students in grades 6 through 8 from government schools across nearby villages.

Healthcare Initiatives

Nyasa annually hosts Sanjeevani - a one day medical health camp with a goal of bringing medical care and health awareness to remote areas.

At Nyasa, we organize community events like Diwali, Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi and many more to bring happiness to Nyasa kids and the community. Through these celebrations, we create memorable experiences and distribute gifts to make every child feel special and valued.
